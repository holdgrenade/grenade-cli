# Showreel

Part of `grenade-cli`: its rules and invariants are in the repo's `CLAUDE.md`. Read PROTOCOL.md "Showreel" first.

A showreel is the day's features as clips of the product in use, with a title and a line each, and no count of anything (Adam: a reel of what shipped, never of the work). The daemon captures nothing itself: **the agent is the camera.** When something a person can see has changed, the agent runs the app, records it, and hands the file over with `grenade clip`. The daemon keeps the clips by day, cuts them into pieces, and at the end of the day announces the reel in Command Center.

## The clip (`src/showreel/clipStore.ts`, `clipFile.ts`, `media.ts`)

- `grenade clip <file> --title "…" [--line "…"] [--before] [--session <id>]` posts the file's absolute path and words to `POST /clips`; the session is `GRENADE_SESSION` (the daemon sets it in every session's tmux) unless named, and is kept only when the daemon lists it. The daemon reads the file itself: nothing is uploaded, the control port is loopback.
- `ClipStore.add`: a recording (`.mov`, `.mp4`, `.m4v`, `.webm`, `.gif`) is re-encoded into `<GRENADE_HOME>/clips/<date>/<id>.mp4` at 720p and at most `CLIP_SECONDS_MAX` (15 s) with ffmpeg when it is on PATH, else the Mac's `avconvert` (`Preset1280x720`); without either, an `.mp4` is kept as it came and anything else refused with a sentence. A picture (`.png`, `.jpg`) is copied. Its length and size come from its own `moov` header (`mp4InfoOf`, pure: the video track's `tkhd` and `mdhd`; the encoders write fast-start files, so it is at the front), else ffprobe, else `mdls` (which knows nothing of a file Spotlight has not seen); a picture's size from its own header (`imageSizeOf`, pure). The clip's `Clip` is written beside it as `<id>.json`, mode 0600 in a folder of mode 0700. The day is the computer's own calendar (`clipDate`). Ids are `c-` and eight letters or digits (`clipIdFrom`). Days older than `KEEP_DAYS` (30) are removed at start.
- `ClipStore.chunk(id, from)` reads up to `CLIP_CHUNK_BYTES` (1 MiB) for `clip.get`; `list(date)` the day's clips (the newest `CLIPS_MAX`); `find(id)` across days.

## The cut (`src/showreel/showreelCut.ts`, `showreelPrompt.ts`, `showreelService.ts`)

- `cutShowreel` is pure. Without a model: clips with the same title (and session) are one part, its first `before` clip beside the rest, closed by the first succeeded `push` entry of a clip's session within two hours of its last clip (`doneOf`); each part is a piece, ordered by its first clip. With a model's proposal: the pieces are the model's (title, line, buckets as parts, a board by file name, a before), every clip or board it names that the day does not have is dropped, a part left with no clip is dropped, and every clip the model left out gets a piece of its own. Every title and line is folded to one line and cut (`foldText`). The opening sentence (`openingOf`) is the model's when it wrote one, else the protocol's `defaultOpening` from the titles; a cut without the model keeps the model's sentence while the titles are the same. Nothing counts anything.
- `ShowreelService.frame(date)` cuts a day again without the model whenever its clips changed since the last cut (`from`, the clip ids the cut was made from) and emits `changed`; `MODEL_SETTLE_MS` (20 s) after the last clip it asks the model (`title`), cuts again with the proposal, saves and emits `changed` again. `make(date)` asks the model now (`grenade showreel --make`). Each day's cut is `<GRENADE_HOME>/showreels/<date>.json` (`ShowreelDoc`: pieces, `from`, `titled`, `announced`), mode 0600.
- The model is `claude -p` with Haiku under `SHOWREEL_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, the summarizer's path (`runClaudePrompt`), so it needs no key and runs with no tools; off with `GRENADE_SUMMARIES=off` or `--no-summaries`, when the agents' own titles stand. Its input (`buildShowreelInput`) fences the clips, the day's boards (`dayBoardsOf`: every canvas of the listed sessions' folders, read with the canvas readers, each board saved that day) and each session's last two replies of the day as data written by agents. Its reply is JSON pieces (`parseShowreelReply`); prose around it is ignored, and no JSON leaves the cut as it was.
- **The end of the day.** Every minute (`HOUR_CHECK_MS`) `checkHour` looks at the clock: at or after the hour in `showreel.json` (`grenade showreel hour <0-23>`, 18 unless set) a day with pieces is titled (once) and announced (once): `TalkService.addRow({ kind: "showreel", text })`, the pieces' titles with " · " between (`showreelRowText`), which reaches every client watching the thread as a `talk.entry`. A clip that arrives after the hour is cut in; the row is not written again.
- `Connection`: `clips` and `showreel` answer at once from the service; a connection that asked for a day's `showreel` gets it again on every `changed` of that day (`showreelDays`), until it closes. `clip.get` answers `clip.chunk` or `bad_frame` with `ref: "clip.get"`. A daemon with no clips folder (a test daemon without a home) says no `showreel: 1` and answers the three frames with `bad_frame`.
- No push notification yet: the row in the thread is the announcement. A sealed "Your showreel is ready" push would add a `PushEvent` and its vectors; not built.

## The video (`src/showreel/render/`)

`grenade showreel render [date] [--portrait] [--fps 30] [--out file]` (`POST /showreel/render`) renders the day's reel to an H.264 MP4 in `~/Movies/Grenade Showreels/<date>.mp4` (`~/Videos/…` on Linux), Canvas 2's R1A and R1B. Nothing leaves the computer, and nothing new is recorded: the video is cut from the same document the apps play.

- `videoTimeline` (pure): the cut on a half-second grid: the open (the mark, the wordmark, "Showreel · Friday 9 October"; 2 s), the opening sentence (2 s), a wall of every clip and board of the day flying by under "Today." (2 s), a chapter per part (2.5 s, or 1.5 s past six pieces: its revision's boards fan out with the chosen one lit, the clip comes forward, a before beside it, the title and line at the foot, the done chip stamps), a montage of what is left when four pictures are, and the close (1.5 s). `videoDay` writes the day; `boardsWanted` picks the day's boards (the named ones and their revisions first, at most 24); `siblingsOf` finds a revision's other boards. No number is written on screen.
- `videoPageHtml` (pure): one self-contained page that draws the timeline at any instant through `window.__showreel.seek(ms)` (a video inside it is seeked to the chapter's time and awaited), so the picture at a time never depends on when it was asked for; `ready()` waits for every picture.
- `ChromePage`: a headless Chrome found on this computer (`resolveChrome`: PATH, the usual app paths, `GRENADE_CHROME`; never downloaded), driven over the DevTools protocol on `ws` (`Page.navigate`, `Runtime.evaluate`, `Page.captureScreenshot`), with file access and a profile folder of its own, stopped when the render ends.
- `renderVideo`: renders each wanted board to a PNG at 1280 × 800 with one Chrome, writes the page and the pictures to a temp folder, then with a second Chrome at 1920 × 1080 (1080 × 1920 with `--portrait`) seeks every frame and pipes the PNGs into ffmpeg (`image2pipe` → libx264, crf 20, faststart). About a minute for 16 s at 30 fps. `RenderError` is a sentence for the owner: no Chrome, no ffmpeg (`brew install ffmpeg`), nothing to render.
- Not yet: sound (the file is silent), the mark's picture (a drawn "G" until the daemon ships one), the apps hosting the same page as their player, a nightly render, and offering the file to a phone (the Mac reads it from Movies).

## The rule the agents follow

The workspace's root `CLAUDE.md` asks every agent, once something visible has changed, to run it and record it before saying it is done: a recording of the feature in use, 5 to 15 seconds, or a screenshot when it does not move, and the old behavior first when it can (`--before`). How to capture on each platform is in that rule (the Mac window with `screencapture`, the iOS simulator with `simctl io recordVideo`, Android with `adb screenrecord`, a page with headless Chrome). Secrets stay off the screen: the daemon keeps what the agent recorded.
